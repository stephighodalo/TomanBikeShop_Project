
<h1>Toman Bike Share Dashboard</h1>

<h2>Overview</h2>

<p>
This project involved developing a business intelligence dashboard for <strong>Toman Bike Share</strong> to analyse revenue, profitability, seasonal performance, and rider demographics. The goal was to transform raw bike-share data into actionable insights that could support business and pricing decisions.
</p>

<p>
The project followed an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from database development and SQL analysis to Power BI dashboard creation and business recommendations.
</p>

![](https://github.com/stephighodalo/TomanBikeShop_Project/blob/main/TomanBike_Dashboard.png)

<h2>Tools Used</h2>

<ul>
<li>SQL</li>
<li>Power BI</li>
<li>Database Management</li>
<li>Data Analysis &amp; Visualisation</li>
<li>Business Intelligence</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Tasks</h2>

<ul>
<li>Built and structured a database for the bike-share data</li>
<li>Developed SQL queries to clean, transform, and analyse the data</li>
<li>Connected the database to Power BI</li>
<li>Created an interactive dashboard for business performance analysis</li>
<li>Analysed hourly revenue and profit trends</li>
<li>Examined seasonal revenue patterns</li>
<li>Analysed rider demographics and customer behaviour</li>
<li>Developed data-driven recommendations for future pricing</li>
</ul>

<h2>Visualisations &amp; Dashboard Analysis</h2>

<p>
The Power BI dashboard includes visualisations covering key business performance areas, including:

<h2>Key Findings</h2>

<p>
The analysis provided insights into revenue performance, profitability, seasonal demand, and rider behaviour. These findings were used to identify opportunities for improving revenue generation and developing a more informed pricing strategy.
</p>

<h2>Recommendation</h2>

<h3>Conservative Price Increase</h3>

<p>
Given the substantial price increase implemented in the previous year, a more measured increase should be considered to reduce the risk of negatively affecting customer demand.
</p>

<p>
A potential <strong>10–15% price increase</strong> could be evaluated as a starting range.
</p>

<p>
If the 2022 price was <strong>$4.99</strong>:
</p>

<ul>
<li>A 10% increase would result in approximately <strong>$5.49</strong></li>
<li>A 15% increase would result in approximately <strong>$5.74</strong></li>
</ul>

<h3>Segmented Pricing</h3>

<p>
Consider different pricing strategies for <strong>casual and registered riders</strong>, as these customer groups may have different usage patterns and price sensitivities.
</p>

<h3>Market Analysis</h3>

<p>
Before implementing a price increase, conduct additional research into customer satisfaction, competitor pricing, market conditions, and the broader economic environment.
</p>

<h3>Monitor &amp; Adjust</h3>

<p>
After implementation, closely monitor revenue, ride volume, customer retention, and customer feedback. This will allow the business to evaluate the impact of the new pricing and make adjustments based on actual customer behaviour.
</p>
